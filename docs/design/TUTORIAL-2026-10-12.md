# App tour — 12 October 2026

Owner brief: using the same two-pass process as the UX review (`UX-REVIEW-2026-10-12.md`), design
and build a first-visit tutorial that shows how the app is used. It should change with the
account's role and cover every feature that role has.

Owner's choices (2026-10-12):

- **Existing staff** are offered it once, like new staff.
- **A promotion** gets a short "what's new for your role" tour of only the parts it adds.

## Design

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| **Point at the app's own chrome**: one stop per sidebar group the role has, then the top bar (search, bell, New), then the account menu. | The sidebar *is* the feature list, already filtered by capability. The tour reads the same `NAV` and `can()`, so it can't show a page the role can't open, and a new page shows up in the tour automatically. A unit test fails if a sidebar place has no description. |
| **Grouped stops**, 5 (officer) to 6 (executive), not one per page. | Product-tour completion falls steeply after about 5 steps (Appcues/Pendo benchmarks). Grouping by the sidebar's own sections keeps it short and maps onto what's on screen. |
| **Worded per role**: `describe(href, role)`. | An officer reads "Loans you look after"; management reads "Every private loan…". The CEO's Staff stop mentions one-time reset links and Settings mentions the backup download. The account stop warns about authenticator codes only for roles with step-up actions. The bell and New menu say what *this* role gets. |
| **Names open the page behind the tour**, which carries on. | It lives in the app layout, so it survives client navigation. "Show, don't tell", without losing your place. |
| **Ends on a first task**: officers get "Price your first loan" (or paste the phone cards); management and executives get "See what needs attention" or the books. | The tour's job is to get someone to their first real action, not to finish a slideshow. |
| **Remembered on the staff record**: migration 0023 adds `staff.tour_completed_at` and `tour_role`. Set by the person's own click (Finish, Not now, Skip, Escape), never on page load. | It works across devices. Like `notification_read`, it's the person's own display state, so it isn't written to the audit log. |
| **Promotions**: `tourToOffer` compares the role with `tour_role`. Only a more senior role is offered a tour, and it lists only places that are new or read differently. | "You're now Management" shows Dashboard, Finance, Staff and so on, but not Borrowers, which reads the same. Moving down or sideways (CEO to COO) offers nothing. |
| **Restartable** from the account menu ("Take the tour"). | Every tour is skippable, so it must be findable again (NN/g). |
| **No tour library**: Base UI Dialog (focus trap, Escape, `role=dialog`) plus a measured spotlight. | Nothing new for the CSP to allow, zero cost, the app's own tokens, and keyboard shortcuts already pause while a dialog is open. |

Accessibility: each stop is a modal dialog named by its title. Focus moves to the title on every
stop, so a screen reader reads it. Arrow keys and Next/Back move between stops. Progress is
written ("Step 2 of 5") as well as drawn. Motion is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`. axe
(WCAG 2.2 A/AA + best practice) passes on the welcome, a stop and the finish card, in light and dark.

## Pass 1 — the designer (on v1)

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **The spotlight had no visible edge.** Tailwind rings are box-shadows, and the dimming shadow replaced them. In the dark sidebar a padded spotlight also showed a sliver of white page beside it. | An outline instead of a ring (brand green, set inside the edge in the sidebar). |
| D2 | **Walls of grey text**: each place got two or three lines of prose, with nothing linking it visually to the highlighted rows. | Each item has the sidebar's own icon; copy cut by about 40% (one line, two at most). |
| D3 | **Two progress indicators** ("Tour · 1 of 5" and dots) and **no visible way out** but a small ×. | A thin progress bar, "Step 1 of 5", and "Skip tour" in the footer. |
| D4 | The welcome promised "a one-minute tour… 5 short stops" without saying what they are. | A numbered agenda of the stops. |
| D5 | The New menu was described as a run of menu labels ("Start a new loan, a new mortgage quote, paste phone cards, paste from tablet or import bank export"). | Verb phrases by what the role can do: "Start a loan or a mortgage quote, paste phone cards or a tablet agreement, and import a bank export." |
| D6 | The finish card offered "See what needs attention" to someone already on the dashboard, so clicking it did nothing. | Suggestions skip the page you're on. |

## Pass 2 — the first-time user (each role, desktop and phone)

| # | Where they got stuck | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| U1 | "It says Collections is where I log calls. Can I see it?" The cards were read-only. | Names are links that open the page behind the tour ("· open now" on the current one). |
| U2 | Clicking the dimmed page to "get to it" **ended the tour and saved it as done**. | Only Escape, ×, Skip tour and Not now close it. |
| U3 | **Phone**: "In the menu", but nothing was highlighted, and the hint showed ☰ when the button is a panel icon. It also mentioned Ctrl K and keyboard shortcuts. | Sidebar stops highlight the real menu button with its icon. "Tap" replaces "click", search says "the magnifier", and there's no shortcuts line. |
| U4 | The bell promised an officer "weeks to close, records to check", which they never get. | Role-specific: officers hear about broken promises; management also gets weeks to close and records to check. |
| U5 | The promotion welcome said "take it again from your account menu", but that menu gives the full tour. | "Under a minute." |
| U6 | After clicking a name, the arrow keys stopped moving between stops, because focus was on the link. Found while writing the browser test. | Arrow keys work anywhere in the card. |

## Files

- `src/components/app-shell/tour-steps.ts`: content, pure (`buildTour`, `tourToOffer`, `welcome`); tests next to it.
- `src/components/app-shell/app-tour.tsx`: welcome, stops and finish; `START_TOUR` event.
- `src/server/account/tour.ts` and `src/app/(app)/tour-action.ts`: remember finished or skipped.
- Anchors: `data-tour="group-<sidebar group>"`, `topbar`, `menu` (the sidebar button, for phones), `account`.
- Tests: `tour-steps.test.ts` (7) and `tests/e2e/tour.spec.ts` (5, including axe). E2E sign-up dismisses the
  welcome (`tests/e2e/support.ts`) so the other specs start without it.

## QA review before shipping

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Which tour to offer was recomputed from the layout's props. A refresh while the finish card was open would bring "done" back as a prop, and the card would vanish. | The offer is decided once, when the app opens. |

Checked and fine:
- Ctrl K during the tour: the search opens on top, and Escape closes only the search.
- Collapsed (icons-only) sidebar.
- A 1280×600 window: the CEO's longest stop fits beside its group, with Next reachable.
- 200% zoom (phone layout).
- Opening a page from a stop, then the top-bar stop: it points at the new page's bar.
- Browser Back mid-tour; Tab stays inside the card.
- Escape saves within 200 ms.
- "Take the tour" on an in-app 404.
