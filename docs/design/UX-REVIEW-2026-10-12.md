# UX review — 12 October 2026

Owner brief: review the app like a conversion-obsessed SaaS founder and designer (Linear, Superhuman,
Vercel, Raycast, Arc as the bar), then click through it as a first-time user and note where they got
confused or wanted to leave; report, then fix. Follows `REDESIGN-2026-10.md` and `UX-PASS-2026-10-11.md`.

There's no free trial in an invite-only staff tool. Here, "conversion" means a new member of staff
finishing their first real task (log a call, price a loan, paste the cards) without asking anyone.
The only public screen is sign-in.

Method: screenshots of all 31 pages as CEO (desktop light, desktop dark, 390 px phone), the auth
pages in both themes and on a phone, then a scripted first-time walk-through. The walk-through went
bell → each "Needs attention" item → Collections → a loan → New loan (filled in as a newcomer
would) → search → paste pages → security → 404.

## Pass 1 — the designer

| # | Finding | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Two filled green buttons on most pages**: the top bar's "Add data" and the page's own "New loan"/"New quote". Two primaries means no primary. "Add data" also held New loan and New quote, which aren't data. | High | The top-bar menu is now an outline **New** button with two labelled groups, *Start a deal* and *Bring in data*. Each page's own primary action is the only filled button. |
| D2 | **Four hand-made tab bars** (Loans, Mortgages, Transactions, Reports) with different padding and weight. Three had **no keyboard focus ring**. Mortgages had no counts while Loans did. | High (a11y, consistency) | One `ViewTabs` component: focus ring, counts everywhere, a red count for work waiting ("Needs review"). |
| D3 | 47 console errors per crawl: every link styled as a button (44 places) went through Base UI's `<button>` primitive. Base UI's only alternative, `nativeButton={false}`, would announce links as buttons. | Medium (a11y, noise) | `Button` with `render={<Link/>}` now renders the link itself with button styles, so it stays a link. A disabled link renders as inert text, so "Newer" on page 1 can't be clicked. |
| D4 | Mortgage status pill **"Terms unknown"** on 26 of 29 rows: grey noise that doesn't say what to do. | Medium | **"Not pasted"** (the same words as the tab and the warning), or "Terms incomplete" when a paste lacked details. |
| D5 | **Sign-in brand panel**: 70% empty charcoal, the wordmark shown twice on one screen, and a spec-sheet footer ("Ledger: Double-entry"). | Medium (first impression) | Logo shown once on wide screens. The panel now says what the app is ("The books behind every loan"), with three plain proof points. The text is no longer hidden from screen readers. |
| D6 | **Commissions shows the same zero three times**: "Owed to staff $0", "Nobody is owed anything" and an "Owed to staff: Nothing yet" card. | Low | The Owed card appears only when someone is owed. "Weeks to close" names the week and links to it; "Payouts to assign" jumps to the payouts. |
| D7 | **Paste boxes' placeholder looked like pasted data** (realistic amounts and wallet numbers in monospace). A newcomer thinks the box is already filled. | Medium | The placeholders now say in words where the copy starts and ends. |
| D8 | **One long note filled the screen** on a borrower's profile, pushing the rest of the history off. | Medium | `LongText`: notes over 400 characters show the start and "Show the whole note" (a native `<details>`). The collections list shortens notes to 160 characters. |
| D9 | A mortgage not yet pasted showed an **Agreement card of 14 dashes**. | Low | One sentence saying the tablet paste fills it in, plus the start date. |
| D10 | Dashboard **"Recent activity" was mostly "Signed in" / "Password accepted"**. | Low | Routine sign-in steps are left out there; failures and security changes still show, and the full audit log is unchanged. |

## Pass 2 — the first-time user

| # | Where they got stuck or wanted to leave | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| U1 | "Assign loan officers — 1 active loans without one" → **landed on the full loan list**, no filter, couldn't find the one loan. | The link opens `?status=live&officer=unassigned` (new **Active & overdue** view, shown as a tab while in use). The Loans "Open loans" figure links there too. The grammar is fixed ("1 open loan"). |
| U2 | Defaulted loan: the schedule showed **"—" for the five missed instalments, the same as future ones**. "So were they paid or not?" | Past-due unpaid instalments on open loans say **Not paid** (red on defaulted loans); the next one due says **Next**. |
| U3 | New loan → Issue contract → **error appeared in the right-hand column** by the buttons, a screen away from the empty field, which wasn't marked. | The field is marked invalid, explained beneath, focused and scrolled to. The message by the buttons says "Check the highlighted field". Every required field is checked in reading order, and server messages map back to their field. |
| U4 | Suspect-mortgage alert said **"merge it … (bottom of this page)"**, but on a desktop the panel is on the right. | Names the panel instead. The "Duplicate record?" panel is hidden on real (banked) records, where it offered nothing to do. |
| U5 | Sign-in: **"Ask a manager to re-issue your invite"**, which is out of date. Password reset links exist, and only the CEO (or above) can issue them. | "Ask the CEO for a one-time reset link." |
| U6 | Collections on a phone: **the amount owed and the follow-up were off-screen** in a sideways-scrolling table. | On phones the borrower cell shows "Owes $X · N missed · not contacted"; those columns appear from `sm` up. |
| U7 | Settings: interest-rate range "**needs Executive Management sign-off**" next to "Warnings only — officers can still issue". Which is it? | "Outside it, a new loan is warned to get Executive Management's agreement (not blocked)." |
| U8 | Commissions: "Weeks to close: 1", but the table row says "2 to fix". **Which week?** | The figure names the week and links to it (D6). |

## Looked at, not changed

- **Status column repeats the tab** on single-status tabs (every row "Active" on the Active tab). It's
  minor, and the column matters on Open, All and Active & overdue.
- **Breadcrumb after Collections → loan reads "Loans"**: the browser's Back returns to the queue.
  A `from=` parameter would add state to every link for little gain.
- **Staff list has no search**: the real team is about 10 people; the 100+ here are test accounts.
- **Dashboard cash $237,938 vs chart "$185,331 now"**: local test data has bank rows dated after today
  (up to 19 Oct). The chart stops at today and the books don't. Real exports are never from the future.
- **32 px controls on phones** (below the 44 px touch guideline): the app is used at a desk alongside
  the game. A phone density is a separate decision for the owner.

## Verification

`pnpm check` (lint, types, 515 unit/integration tests, build). The browser suite has 25 tests, all
passing, including axe WCAG 2.2 A/AA + best practice in both themes, no sideways scrolling at 390 px,
the four role crawls with zero console errors, and `tests/e2e/ux-review.spec.ts` (6 tests for the
fixes above). Before/after screenshots were checked by eye at desktop and phone width.

## QA review before shipping (12 October 2026)

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | **"Not paid" was too eager.** It counted only regular bank instalments, so it would also flag an instalment that was paid late (`late_principal`), one covered by an early repayment, one due after the latest bank export, and one the game hadn't charged yet (it charges a few hours after the due time). | An instalment is "Not paid" only if it isn't covered by regular or late payments or the phone card's progress, and it fell due over a day before the newest bank row. Any early repayment turns the marker off. |
| Q2 | Shortening a long note could cut an emoji in half and show a broken character. | `shorten()` counts whole characters (unit-tested). |

Checked and fine:
- Every `Button render={…}` use (36 links, 2 downloads), including a disabled one ("Newer" on page 1 is inert text; it's a link from page 2).
- The `live` loans filter and its Excel export.
- The schedule on all 30 open loans in the local data. The one remaining "Not paid" on an active loan is genuine: due 11 Oct, no payment, phone card pasted before the due date.

Pre-existing, not changed: a mistyped top-level address (e.g. `/no-such-page`) shows Next's plain 404 outside the
app frame. In-app 404s (an unknown loan, say) use the app's own page.
