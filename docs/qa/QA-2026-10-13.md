# QA — 13 October 2026: the app against the phone, the tablet and the bank

Owner brief: check every value the app shows against the phone records and the bank exports, find where they
don't match, and fix it. Two bugs reported the same day started it: bank times shown 5 hours late (ADR 0004) and
Onyx Greystone's loan showing −$156 where her phone card said $156 left.

## Method

- **Sources as the oracle.** The bank rows (every export, whose header balances reconcile to the dollar), each
  loan's latest phone card and each mortgage's latest tablet agreement, as stored verbatim in production. Read-only
  queries only.
- **The app's own figures.** The same domain functions the pages use (`analyseLoan`, `analyseMortgage`, `stateAt`,
  `listLoans`, the reports), so a mismatch is what a user would see.
- **As of the paste.** A card or agreement is compared with the bank as it stood when it was pasted; later rows
  are ignored.
- **Independent parsing.** The card's raw text is re-read with separate regular expressions to check the parser.
- **Every finding explained** against the raw rows before it was called a bug (loans probed one by one).
- The checks are a reusable script: `scripts/audit/e-sources.ts` (see `scripts/audit/README.md`). The ledger audits
  (`a-ledger` … `f-bugs`) were re-run alongside.

## Later the same day: a renamed customer imported twice

Reported by Zion Pemberton at 03:58 UK: "It thinks there's like 300k in the account … Cyan changed her name … it
thought there was another mortgage in there."

**Cause.** The bank export prints each person's *current* name on all their rows, past ones included. The import
recognised a row it already had by a hash of every raw field, the initiator's name among them. Cyan Oakhurst became
Cyan Spencer, so her two rows already in the books (29 Sep "118 Imagination Ct: Down Payment & Fees" $199,679 and
6 Oct "Loan Repayment" $15,602) looked new in the 02:55 UTC export and were posted again. The mortgage sync made a
second mortgage from the repeated down payment and linked the repeated repayment to the first one; the 03:12 tablet
paste then matched the duplicate. The preview did show the $183,835 difference, but its wording explained it away
("expected if this export doesn't start from the account's first transaction"). No other renamed rows in production.

**Effect** (production, read-only check): cash $322,365 against the bank's $138,530; 1110 clearing −$183,835; the tax
memo $31,446 too high (the 1 Oct State record looked $31,017 short); 30 mortgages instead of 29 (mortgage book,
borrower 16754, officer counts, "Can we lend?", up to $33,499 of broker interest to come counted twice); the original
mortgage with the 6 Oct repayment twice (paid so far $230,883 against the tablet's $215,281). Not affected: P&L,
commissions (before the scheme), loans and levies, the State allowance (both rows predate the first reading), the
audit chain.

**Fix.**
- Import: rows are matched on every raw field except the name (`identityKey`, `alreadyStored` in
  `domain/bank/fingerprint.ts`); the State ID stays in, so another person's identical payment is still new. Stored
  fingerprints are unchanged.
- Preview: the balance is checked as at the export's last row (exact whatever order exports arrive in), and a
  difference is a red warning, as are new rows dated before the latest row in the books. Warnings only (owner).
- Migration 0028: rows identical to a row from an earlier import except for the name are removed with their own
  postings (owner: remove, not reverse — they are the same bank transaction twice); a mortgage made from one is
  retired into the original's, which takes the newer tablet paste; the batch's counts are corrected. It stops,
  changing nothing, if a duplicate was reclassified, paired, linked to a loan or counted for commission. Production
  preview: exactly the 2 rows, $183,835 of cash and $31,446 of tax memo; all guards clear.
- Tests: `fingerprint.test.ts`, `tests/integration/renamed-import.test.ts` (re-import under a new name adds nothing;
  the red warnings; the migration on a reproduction of the double import, and again with nothing to do),
  `tests/e2e/renamed-import.spec.ts`. 545 tests + 36 browser.

## Later the same day: next payments 1 h 45 m late after a repeated paste

The re-run above flagged three active loans (Greer Marlowe, Oakley Coldwell, Frankie Yardley) whose phone card put the next
payment 1–1.5 h after the game's due date from the bank (E1.6).

**Cause.** At 02:23 UTC all four tabs were pasted with exactly the text pasted at 00:38 (identical hashes): one copy,
pasted twice, 1 h 45 m apart. The phone only gives a countdown ("Next payment: in 23 hours"), and the app counted it
from the paste, so every active loan's stored next payment moved 1 h 45 m late. The game's due dates were right: the
00:38 cards agree with them on all 13 active loans. The loan page's "Next payment" (Oakley Coldwell showed 14 Oct, the
game's is 13 Oct 22:50 UTC), the loan details, My work, the loans export and the forecast's first instalment use the
stored time. Day countdowns ("in 4 days") only ever gave the day, not the time.

**Fix.**
- The loan sync sets a running loan's next payment from the bank: the game's due date for the next instalment
  (`nextPaymentDue`), exact and moved on as soon as a payment is imported. The card's countdown is used only while
  the bank is behind the phone (the phone counts more instalments than the bank has) or the loan has no bank start.
- A paste with the same card text as that loan's previous card doesn't move its next payment.
- The paste preview warns when a tab is exactly the text of its previous paste although its countdowns should have
  moved since (`staleRepaste`): "Open the tab on the phone and copy it again."
- `e-sources` E1.6 counts a countdown from when its text was first pasted, and doesn't compare the app's next payment
  with a card the bank has moved past. Production after the fix: 13 checked, 0 card mismatches; the four stored
  times from the repeated paste (also Arden Delacroix's) are corrected by the next import or paste.
- Tests: `nextPaymentDue` on the three loans' real times, `staleRepaste`, the sync and a repeated paste in
  `loans.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/repeat-paste.spec.ts`. 552 tests + 37 browser.

## Known differences, left as they are

- **The phone's "remaining" after a missed payment** is the principal still owed plus the game's 2% on every
  instalment still to pay (Winter: $20,000 × 1.02 = $20,400). The app's "Still owed" is the principal ($20,000); the
  penalty becomes a late fee as each instalment is paid. The loan page shows both and says which includes it.
- The +2% on the *interest* row of a penalised instalment was always interest income (4000); unchanged.
- **Defaulted cards' "remaining"** includes the penalties (+4% after three misses) — the phone's figure now shows
  beside the books'.
- **The game can move a loan to Default before the third miss** (Zion Merriweather: 2 missed, already in Default on
  9 Oct). The app dates the default to when the phone first showed it — the latest it can know.
- **Default cards keep counting misses after the last instalment** (Onyx Everhart: 7/8 paid, "3 missed").
